## Primary persona (example: replace with your real person)
**Nasreen, 58, Karachi.** Homemaker. Has a Meezan Bank account and the app on her phone, which her son installed.
- **Today:** calls her son every time she needs to send money or check the balance. Avoids the app alone.
- **Fears:** "Agar ghalat button dab gaya to paise chalay jayenge." Afraid of looking foolish when asking again.
- **Language:** reads Urdu comfortably, English menu words only partly. Types Roman Urdu on WhatsApp. Prefers voice notes.
- **Habits:** uses WhatsApp daily, ATM monthly. Receives "BISP inaam" and "account block" SMS often.
- **Goal:** send money to her son and pay the bijli bill without calling anyone.

## How each pain point maps to the app
| Pain point (observed) | Feature |
|---|---|
| Afraid of pressing the wrong button | Mashq: fake app, forgiving errors, "Kyun?" explanations |
| English menu labels | Assistant quotes the exact English label inside Urdu instructions |
| Calls family for every task | Assistant step-by-step + recap so next time she does it alone |
| Fake OTP calls / prize SMS | OTP-call rehearsal in Mashq, Fraud check, scam quiz |
| "Bank ne paise kyun kaatay?" | Statement samjhein: every fee in plain Urdu |
| Forgets why cash was withdrawn | Kharcha diary + month-end matching |

## Interview guide (15 min, record with consent, no account details)
1. Last time you used your banking app alone, what happened?
2. What do you do when you get stuck? Whom do you call?
3. Which words in the app confuse you? (show the home screen)
4. Have you received a message asking for an OTP or prize? What did you do?
5. Rate 1–5: how confident are you sending money alone?
